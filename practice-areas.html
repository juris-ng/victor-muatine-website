Employment Law

Losing your job unfairly is devastating. The firm fights to ensure employers are held accountable and workers are treated with dignity.

Kenya's Employment Act and the Constitution provide robust protections for workers — but enforcing those rights requires a lawyer who knows the Employment and Labour Relations Court and is not afraid to take on large employers. The firm represents both individual employees and organisations in employment matters, with a track record of securing reinstatement orders and substantial compensation awards.

  • Unfair dismissal claims
  • Redundancy disputes

  • Workplace discrimination
  • Sexual harassment claims
  • Salary & benefit disputes
