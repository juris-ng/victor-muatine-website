Who We Are

A firm founded on
fearless advocacy

Victor Muatine & Co. Advocates was founded with a single conviction: that every person who walks through our door — regardless of the size of their matter — deserves the same fierce, strategic, and meticulous representation that the most powerful interests in Kenya receive.

Victor came to national prominence when a video of his courtroom performance at the Kisumu Children's Court went viral — not because of theatrics, but because of the clarity, precision, and conviction with which he argued. That video was not an anomaly. It was a window into how we approach every single case.

Today the firm operates from offices in Nairobi and Kitui, serving clients across Kenya in matters ranging from complex commercial litigation to sensitive family disputes, criminal defence, and land law. We are admitted to practice before all courts of Kenya, including the Court of Appeal.

What We Stand For

Three principles that
guide everything we do

These are not words on a wall. They are the standards by which every advocate at this firm is measured, every day.

01

Integrity Without Compromise

We do not tell clients what they want to hear. We tell them what they need to hear — honestly, clearly, and without embellishment. Our advice is grounded in the law as it stands, not in false optimism.

We never take a matter we cannot give our full attention, and we never make a promise we cannot keep.

Principle One

02

Strategy Before Speed

Every matter receives a deliberate strategy before a single document is filed. We think before we move — and when we move, we move with purpose.

This means clients are never surprised. They know the plan, they understand the risks, and they are prepared for every outcome.

Principle Two

03

Results Measured by Outcomes

We do not measure success by hours billed or filings submitted. We measure it by the outcomes we secure for our clients — and those outcomes are what our reputation is built on.

Win, settle advantageously, or set the strongest possible foundation for appeal — that is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Principle Three

Our Journey

Key milestones in
the firm's history

2013

Called to the Bar

Victor Muatine admitted as an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya upon completion of his KSLP Diploma.

2015

Firm Founded

Victor Muatine & Co. Advocates opens its first office in Kitui, serving the local community with dispute resolution and family law matters.

2018

Nairobi Office Opens

A second office launches in Nairobi, expanding the firm's reach into commercial litigation and corporate advisory work.

2021

Viral Courtroom Moment

Victor's argument in a paternity case at Kisumu Children's Court goes viral, bringing national attention to the firm's advocacy standard.

2023

Team Expansion

The firm grows to six advocates, adding specialists in employment, land, environmental, and criminal law.

2025

Court of Appeal Admission

Victor Muatine formally admitted to practice before the Court of Appeal, completing the firm's full-court practice capability across all levels of the Kenyan judiciary.
