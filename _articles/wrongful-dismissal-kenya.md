## What is Wrongful Dismissal?

Under the **Employment Act 2007**, every employee is entitled to **fair termination**. A dismissal is wrongful — or unfair — when an employer:

- Terminates without valid reason
- Fails to follow a fair procedure
- Dismisses an employee for exercising a legal right (e.g. joining a union)
- Terminates without proper notice or pay in lieu of notice

## The Fair Procedure Requirement

Before dismissing an employee for misconduct or poor performance, an employer **must**:

1. Inform the employee of the allegations in writing
2. Give the employee a reasonable opportunity to respond
3. Hold a fair hearing
4. Consider the employee's response before making a decision

Skipping any of these steps — even if the underlying reason is valid — can make the dismissal procedurally unfair.

## Your Remedies

If you have been wrongfully dismissed, you can file a claim at the **Employment and Labour Relations Court**. Remedies include:

- **Reinstatement** to your former position
- **Compensation** of up to 12 months' gross salary
- **Unpaid wages and terminal dues** — leave pay, notice pay, and severance

## Time Limits

You have **3 years** from the date of dismissal to file your claim. Do not delay — evidence becomes harder to gather and witnesses harder to find.
