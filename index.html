The Advocate

More Than
An Advocate

Victor Muatine is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, holding a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from the University of Nairobi and a Postgraduate Diploma from the Kenya School of Law. He founded Victor Muatine & Co. Advocates on a singular conviction: that every client deserves bold, fearless representation — regardless of who stands on the other side.

Known for his passionate courtroom presence and sharp legal mind, Victor has appeared before courts across Kenya in dispute resolution, family law, and commercial litigation. The firm's ethos is simple — Integrity. Strategy. Results.
